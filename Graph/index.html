THE NEURO-SYMBOLIC GATE

AI proposes.
WASM proves.

AI assistants can draft claims, but they cannot certify citations or detect conflicts on their own. DKG Graph Local puts every draft through a deterministic WASM verification gate before anything enters sealed findings.

01 · INPUTS SUB-GRAPH Unverified Drafts

Ingested files, Review Inbox candidates, and AI-proposed claims land in inputs as unverified drafts — never mixed with trusted facts.

02 · WORKER SANDBOX Deterministic WASM Checks

TypeScript-to-WASM programs run in isolated node:worker_threads to validate citations, detect contradictions, and block leaked secrets.

03 · FINDINGS & AUDIT Sealed Graph Nodes

Passing claims are sealed into findings with Merkle roots, EIP-712 digests, and tamper-evident execution receipts in audit.

TWO-STAGE VERIFICATION

Unverified drafts stay isolated.
Only verified facts seal.

Whether authored in Simple Mode, generated by an optional local model, or sent from external tools, claims cannot promote themselves into verified memory.

Stage plain-English proposals safely in inputs.

LOCAL-FIRST CONTINUITY

100% private on 127.0.0.1.
No wallet or gas required.

Everything runs locally on your machine by default — SQLite, Oxigraph Working Memory, WASM verification, and the deterministic offline proposer (or optional local Ollama) need zero TRAC tokens, zero gas fees, and zero external cloud accounts. If you ever choose to publish a permanent public Knowledge Asset, the separate Wallet & Publish tab protects keys with an OS Keychain / Windows Credential Manager-backed AES-256-GCM wallet master key (via native keyring + in-memory k256 EIP-191 signing, with local keyfile fallback) and inspects every triple in a Privacy Review modal before signing.

Public DKG publishing to NeuroWeb, Gnosis, or Base testnets and mainnets is strictly opt-in and never runs automatically.
