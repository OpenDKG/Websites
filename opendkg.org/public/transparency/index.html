Trust and attribution

How to read our claims.

This site explains what each project is, who owns it, and what it does not do. It is not a certification, and it is not a promise of protection.

What we publish

We publish high-level capabilities, ownership, licenses, links, and limits. We do not publish private source, private screenshots, customer data, or unverified metrics. When a claim depends on a vendor’s documentation, we say so.

Ownership and marks

OpenDKG is an independent open-source initiative. Axiom is an independent project documented in the OpenDKG portfolio. Guardian Agent Blackbox is a product of Umanitek. OriginTrail, TRAC, and Hermes are third-party names and marks. Their use here identifies the relevant project or integration; it does not imply a partnership or endorsement.

The OpenDKG name, icon, and logo are used to identify this initiative. Product names and third-party assets remain subject to their owners’ policies.

Axiom and Blackbox

The Axiom package description reflects the project’s launch direction: Axiom includes Guardian Agent Blackbox as an optional agent guardrail. Blackbox is built and maintained by Umanitek, with its own repository, license, documentation, and release process.

This is a statement about the package direction, not an independent test of Blackbox’s runtime behavior. For current behavior, read Umanitek’s documentation. For Axiom’s platform and ownership boundaries, read the public project notes.

What we do not claim

  • We do not claim that any tool stops every attack or every risky agent action.
  • We do not claim certification, audit, or compliance status for any project.
  • We do not invent customers, testimonials, metrics, or partnerships.
  • We do not treat an optional guardrail as operating-system enforcement.
  • We do not describe a planned integration as a verified implementation.

Corrections

If a claim is wrong or out of date, contact the maintainers through the OpenDKG organization. Include the page and the source that should be corrected. Do not post private data, credentials, or personal information in a public issue.
