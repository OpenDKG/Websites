TRACaBot: a moderator’s helper for Telegram scam patterns.

An anti-scam bot that watches joins, records evidence privately, and can share confirmed scam signals across communities.

Independent · MIT Telegram · OpenClaw · DKG shared memory

Read the public history ↗

Default behavior

It observes quietly.

On a join, TRACaBot can run a background lookup against a privacy-preserving identity token. The default outcome is silent observe and log: the bot records the check privately and posts nothing to the group.

Manual commands such as start, scan, and report are optional. The bot does not need a language model or an API key for the default join check.

Sees
Telegram joins and configured community activity.
Can do
Observe, log, flag, and share confirmed scam signals.
Sends
Evidence-backed signals to shared memory, subject to its documented privacy rules.
Does not
Guarantee scam detection, ban on suspicion alone, or replace human moderation.

Enforcement

Removal is opt-in and evidence-gated.

Automatic removal is a separate setting. Even when it is enabled, removal requires an exact stable-identity match, not a display name or similarity match.

It also requires confirmed evidence or an explicit admin review, a fresh record with no newer correction, the configured number of independent confirmed roots, bot permissions, and a target who is not an admin, owner, or bot.

Shared and verified-graph records are flag-only in the installed adapter today. They can prompt a review; they cannot authorize a removal by themselves.

Unknown is not safe or guilty

Graph outages, missing provenance, stale evidence, retractions, and ambiguous lineage fail closed to a private local record. That is a safety boundary, not a verdict.

Model confidence and behavior flags are evidence signals. They are not proof.

Limits

What it cannot prove.

A community moderation tool should be clear about the difference between a signal and a decision.

It cannot guarantee detection

Scam patterns change. A clean check does not prove a person is trustworthy, and a flag is not a conviction.

It cannot replace moderators

Humans still own the policy, the appeals, and the decision to remove someone. The bot is a helper, not an authority.

It cannot verify every publisher

The installed adapter does not expose authenticated publisher-approval provenance for every shared record. That is why shared records are flag-only.

It cannot see every platform

TRACaBot is a Telegram tool. Its evidence and enforcement rules do not automatically transfer to other platforms.
