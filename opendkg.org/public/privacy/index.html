Privacy Policy

Local by default. Clear about every connection.

OpenDKG websites set no cookies or analytics, and Axiom collects no telemetry or user accounts. This notice explains the limited network connections that occur when you visit our pages or turn on optional features.

Effective date: 2026-11-25

1. Scope and controller

This Privacy Policy applies to the public website opendkg.org and every project site served under it — /axiom/, /desktop/, /graph/ and /veritytrail/ — and to the Axiom desktop application (Windows, Linux, and macOS), published by the independent OpenDKG open-source initiative (contact@opendkg.org). Sections 5–7 describe the data flows of the OpenDKG Desktop, DKG Graph Local and VerityTrail applications, which are in development and not yet publicly released.

Independent third-party services or optional integrations—including Umanitek Blackbox, OriginTrail / DKG nodes, Cloudflare, GitHub, YouTube, VirusTotal, and external DNS or AI providers you configure—are operated separately under their own privacy policies and terms.

2. Websites (opendkg.org and all project sites under it)

  • No cookies or analytics: Our websites do not set tracking cookies, advertising identifiers, or behavioral analytics scripts.
  • Hosting and security logs: The sites are served as static pages via Cloudflare Pages, which processes standard HTTP request metadata (such as IP address, timestamp, requested URL, and user-agent) solely to deliver pages and protect against abuse and DDoS attacks.
  • Embedded video previews: On /axiom/, embedded videos use click-to-load thumbnails hosted locally. No connection is made to YouTube (youtube-nocookie.com) unless you click Play.
  • External links: Links to GitHub, DKG Swarm, TRACaBot, and third-party sites leave our pages; those destinations are governed by their own policies.
  • Privacy-notice preference (browser local storage): When you dismiss the privacy notice, the sites store opendkg_privacy_notice_v1 = 1 in your browser's localStorage so the notice stays dismissed. It contains no identifier, is never sent to us, and you can remove it by clearing site data for opendkg.org in your browser.

3. Axiom desktop application

  • No telemetry or accounts: Axiom does not include usage telemetry, analytics, crash-reporting pipelines, ads, or account registration. OpenDKG does not operate a central server that receives your browsing history, firewall rules, prompts, or file contents.
  • Local storage: Network flow history (axiom.db), agent hook evidence metadata (agent-evidence.jsonl), firewall baselines, and local settings remain on your machine. You can inspect, clear, or uninstall them at any time.

4. Optional and user-controlled network features in Axiom

When core local monitoring runs, Axiom makes no outbound telemetry connections. Network connections occur only for the features below according to your settings and installation choice:

  • Blackbox & OriginTrail DKG threat sharing (consented / toggleable): During Complete installation or First-Run setup, or via the toggles on the Agent Protection page and Blackbox Configure dialog, you may choose whether to enable Blackbox automatic community threat sharing or submit manual verified IOC reports. Shared reports contain sanitized/redacted threat sightings and indicators only; local custom rules, secrets, and private prompts are excluded by design. Public CC0 DKG replicas may retain published sightings even after retraction. Turning automatic sharing off keeps all local agent blocking functional without publishing sightings.
  • Update checks: When enabled (on by default; can be turned off under Settings → Updates), Axiom queries GitHub Releases (api.github.com/repos/OpenDKG/Axiom/releases/latest) at startup, every 6 hours, or when you click Check for updates now. No installation ID, hardware identifier, or credentials are sent; GitHub receives standard HTTP request metadata and the product version User-Agent header.
  • Privacy Shield DNS (opt-in / Complete install): When enabled, non-blocked DNS queries are forwarded to the upstream resolver you select (such as Cloudflare DNS-over-HTTPS at 1.1.1.1 or a custom resolver), and configured public blocklists are downloaded when refreshed.
  • Opt-in external integrations (off by default):
    • VirusTotal (VT Check): Sends only the SHA-256 hash of a selected file when you manually request a lookup, using the API key you provide; files are never uploaded.
    • Community feed: Fetches indicators only from the HTTPS feed URL you enable.
    • AI Analyst: Uses local Ollama on your machine by default. If you configure an external LLM provider, only the prompt and redacted network snapshot you explicitly submit are sent to that provider.

5. OpenDKG Desktop application

  • Local by default: The AI gateway listens only on 127.0.0.1. Agent memory, usage records and settings stay on your machine; API keys you add are kept in your operating system's keychain.
  • Cloud model routing (your choice): In the Private Local policy nothing leaves your PC. If you add your own provider keys or choose Smart Saver or Verified Cloud routing, the prompts sent through that route go to the AI provider you selected, under that provider's terms. Secret redaction runs before cloud calls.
  • Marketplace sharing (off by default): If you enable marketplace publishing or provider mode, the local models you choose to expose can be called by marketplace buyers; their requests and your responses pass through the marketplace network. Withdrawing stops new sharing.
  • Wallet and on-chain records: Wallet transfers and any DKG or blockchain anchors you commit are public and immutable once committed; they cannot be deleted later.

6. DKG Graph Local application

  • Local by default: Your documents, the knowledge vault, Working Memory and the MCP endpoint stay on your machine (127.0.0.1). Local AI uses a loopback runtime such as Ollama unless you explicitly allow a remote endpoint.
  • Optional DKG bridge and publishing: Only the assets you choose to bridge or publish leave your device. Published Knowledge Assets on OriginTrail DKG networks are public and, once anchored on-chain, immutable.
  • AI clients you connect: AI assistants you connect over MCP can read the vault content their tools return; what they do with it is governed by those clients' own terms.

7. VerityTrail application

  • Local by default: Scans, SBOMs, findings and reports stay on your machine.
  • Cloud AI providers (opt-in): If you enable a cloud AI provider, the material you submit for an AI audit is sent to that provider under its terms; local servers and CLI harnesses keep the data on your machine.
  • Optional DKG Working Memory and Blackbox (off by default): When enabled, receipts are written only after raw code, file paths and secrets are blocked; the Blackbox reviewer guard is read-only.

8. Contact emails

If you email contact@opendkg.org, we process your email address and message contents solely to read and respond to your inquiry, and we do not use it for marketing or share it with third parties unless legally required.

9. Regional privacy disclosures (EU, Switzerland, and United States)

European Union & EEA (GDPR)

  • Legal bases: Under Art. 6(1) GDPR, optional threat sharing, opt-in integrations (VirusTotal, external AI providers, community feeds), and click-to-load video playback rely on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), which you may withdraw at any time in Axiom settings or by not loading optional content. Standard website transport security (Cloudflare Pages) and replying to correspondence rely on legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Your rights: Where OpenDKG holds personal data (for example, emails sent to contact@opendkg.org), you have the right to request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability, or object to processing, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with your local EU/EEA data protection supervisory authority. Local data stored on your own device can be viewed, exported, or deleted directly by you.

Switzerland (revFADP / nDSG)

  • Transparency and cross-border transfers: In accordance with Art. 19 of the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (revFADP), please note that Cloudflare (website delivery), GitHub (release checks), YouTube (if you click Play on embedded videos), and optional third-party integrations or decentralized OriginTrail DKG peers operate infrastructure outside Switzerland (including the EU and United States).
  • Your rights: You may request information about or deletion of any correspondence held by OpenDKG and have the right to contact the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC / EDÖB).

United States (CCPA/CPRA & Federal Transparency)

  • We do not sell personal information and do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, nor do we collect sensitive personal information or track users across third-party apps or websites.
  • California and other US state residents may contact contact@opendkg.org to request access to or deletion of any email correspondence held by OpenDKG.

10. Children

Our websites and software are general-audience technical tools and are not directed to children under 13 (or under the applicable age of digital consent in the EU/EEA or Switzerland). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

11. Changes and contact

We may update this Privacy Policy as features evolve and will update the effective date above. For privacy questions or requests, email contact@opendkg.org. For technical issues, please open a public issue at GitHub without including personal data, credentials, or private paths.
